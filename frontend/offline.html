Explore your interests

Realistic
Practical activities, tools, building and outdoor tasks.
Investigative
Exploring questions, analysing information and scientific thinking.
Artistic
Creative expression, design, writing and original ideas.
Social
Helping, teaching and supporting other people.
Enterprising
Leading projects, persuading and organising business activities.
Conventional
Organising records, accuracy and clear processes.

These descriptions are prompts for exploration. They do not establish ability or limit what you can become.

Plan your next conversation

  1. Write down activities you enjoy and questions you have.
  2. Discuss your subject choices and improvement targets with a teacher.
  3. Check an institution’s current official programme and admission information.
  4. Confirm funding terms, application dates and required documents with the provider.
  5. Arrange safe practical learning activities through a trusted adult.
